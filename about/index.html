ABOUT JEREMIAH

About me

I’m Jeremiah Richard, from Katy, Texas, in the Greater Houston area. I’m a mechanical engineering graduate and am now studying robotics at Northwestern University.

I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering with a math minor at Hardin-Simmons University, where I also played collegiate football. Balancing engineering classes, team projects, practices, and games was a big part of my undergraduate experience. I graduated in spring 2026 and am now pursuing my M.S. in Robotics at Northwestern.

What I want to work on

I want to build robots that make everyday life easier, whether that means taking care of a chore or helping someone do something they physically can’t do on their own. That’s what draws me to service and assistive robotics, and to a full-stack approach that brings together mechanical design, electronics, and software. I’d like a career where I can help take an idea through building and testing until it becomes something people can use. Long term, I hope to start a business bringing some of my own ideas to life.
